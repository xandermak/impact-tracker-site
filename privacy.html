Privacy policy

Your data stays on your computer.

Last updated October 27, 2026

Impact Tracker is a Chrome extension. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We (the developer) never receive any of your data, so we can't sell it, share it or lose it.

What the extension stores, on your computer only

  • Time per website, per day. Only the site name (for example "youtube.com") and the number of seconds, never full addresses or page content. Used to estimate your browsing footprint.
  • AI prompt estimates, if you turn on AI tracking. The number of prompts and an estimate of reply length for each supported AI site. Never the text of your prompts or the replies.
  • "Checked used first" records. For each product where you click a used option: Amazon's product ID and the difference between its new and used price. Kept per month.
  • Usage counts shown on the "Tester stats" page, such as how often the popup was opened.

Everything is saved with Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Daily records older than about 400 days are deleted automatically. "Reset data" in the popup deletes all of it, and removing the extension deletes it too.

What the extension reads

  • Amazon product pages. When you open the popup on an Amazon.com product page, or on every product page if you turn on Amazon ratings, it reads the title, price, category, brand, fabric and any used offer to work out the estimate. This happens on your computer and isn't stored, except as described above.
  • AI sites, only if you turn on AI tracking. It notices when you send a prompt and how much text appears in reply, to estimate energy use. It doesn't store or send what you write.
  • Tab addresses. To measure time per site, it checks which site is in the active tab or playing audio, and keeps only the site name.

Permissions

Access to Amazon.com and to AI sites is optional. Chrome asks you before it's granted, and turning the feature off gives the access back. The extension never asks for access to all websites.

Links to other stores

"Find it used" links open another store's search, such as eBay or Back Market, with a shortened product name in the address. Nothing is sent until you click. Once you're on that site, its own privacy policy applies.

Children

Impact Tracker isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the extension ever starts sending any data off your computer, it will ask for your permission first.
