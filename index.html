Free Chrome extension

Check before you buy.

Impact Tracker shows the hidden footprint of what you buy on Amazon, and points you to the cheaper, lower-impact option: buying it used.

Coming soon to Chrome

Free · No account · Nothing leaves your computer

Right on the page

See the used price first.

Turn on Amazon ratings and every product page gets a small rating in the corner. When Amazon sells the item used or renewed for less, it says so, along with how much you'd save.

One click searches the right secondhand stores for that product: Back Market and Swappa for phones, Poshmark and thredUP for clothing, your library and ThriftBooks for books, and eBay or Facebook Marketplace for almost everything else.

Your choices add up

See how many products you checked used first this month, and what you could have saved.

Honest numbers

Every estimate has a confidence level and a cited source. We never claim you saved CO₂, because we can't see what you buy.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no servers. Everything is worked out and stored on your own computer.

Clothing scores

Know what you're wearing.

Clothing with a listed fabric gets a 0–100 score from Ecobalyse, the French government's official method for the environmental cost of textiles. It counts climate, water, land, pesticides and microplastics, and tells you in plain words what drives the result.

Your online footprint

Browsing, calls and AI, too.

Impact Tracker also estimates the CO₂ of your browsing, streaming and video calls, and, if you turn it on, the energy, CO₂ and water behind your prompts on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 15 other AI tools.

Weekly recap

Share your week.

One click saves an image of your week to post anywhere. Nothing is uploaded.
